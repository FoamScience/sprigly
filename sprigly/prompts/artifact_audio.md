Teach one thing well: {{topic}}.

Depth {{depth}} — {{scope}}. Stay at that level: do not zoom out to a survey of the whole field,
and do not drift into neighbouring topics. One idea, followed properly, beats five mentioned.

Assume a curious listener who is comfortable with technical material and does not need motivating.
Skip the throat-clearing, the "welcome back", and the recap of what you are about to say. Open on
the substance.

Where the sources disagree, say so and say which is better supported. Where something is a
convention rather than a result, say that too. Name the mechanism, not just the conclusion — the
listener wants to know *why* it works, and what breaks when an assumption fails.

Do not invent examples, numbers or citations that are not in the sources.
